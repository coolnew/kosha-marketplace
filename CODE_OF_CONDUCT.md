# Code of conduct

Be respectful, assume good faith, and discuss ideas and code rather than
attacking people. Welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience
levels. Give actionable feedback and respect people's time and boundaries.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable in issues, pull requests,
discussions, or other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to Neeraj Sharma at **neerajsharma.9@outlook.com**.
Include relevant links and context; share only the personal information needed
to understand the report. If a report concerns the maintainer, you may use
GitHub's platform reporting tools.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or
restrict participation temporarily or permanently. They will consider severity,
context, and repeated behavior, explain decisions where appropriate, and limit
disclosure of private reports. A participant may request reconsideration by
email with additional context.
